## Why this file is separate from the roadmap checkboxes

The checkbox in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) means *"the note exists and I've read it."*
That's a one-way door — once written, always written.

**Confidence is different: it decays.** You can be 🟢 on JWT validation in week 5 and 🟡
by week 9 without touching the file. Tracking the two in the same column hides that, and
the whole point of a study repo is to know what to revise *today*, not what you once read.

---

## Confidence scale

| | Meaning | Action |
|:--:|---|---|
| 🔴 | Couldn't explain it out loud without notes | Re-read the whole note |
| 🟡 | Could explain it, but would fumble a follow-up | Re-read TL;DR + Traps, then say it out loud |
| 🟢 | Could teach it, and handle "why" twice in a row | Leave alone until the next review interval |

**The test for 🟢 is speaking, not reading.** If you haven't said it out loud, you're 🟡.
This is the single most common self-assessment error in interview prep — recognition
feels like recall, and it isn't. You will discover the gap in the interview otherwise.

---

## Spaced repetition schedule

After a topic first reaches 🟢, review it at increasing intervals:

**Day 1 → Day 3 → Day 7 → Day 16 → Day 35**

A review is 60 seconds: read the TL;DR, say the answer to one interview question out
loud, done. If you fumble, drop it back to 🟡 and restart the interval.

Anything still 🔴 in the final week: either fix it or accept you'll say
*"I haven't worked with that directly, but my understanding is…"* — which is a perfectly
good answer, and infinitely better than bluffing. Interviewers are testing calibration as
much as knowledge.
